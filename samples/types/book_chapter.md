# Chapter 3: The long winter

Mara had not seen the river freeze before. She stood at the bank where the water used to move and pressed her boot against the new ice, feeling it hold, then feeling it give a little, the way a promise gives before it breaks.

Her grandmother had told her the winters used to be softer. Mara did not believe her, the way children do not believe in a world that existed before them, until the morning the pipes cracked and the old woman only nodded, as if the cold had finally agreed with her.
