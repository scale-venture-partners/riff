# Support automation pilot: results and recommendation

We recommend expanding the support automation pilot to all consumer queues in Q4.

Over eight weeks the pilot handled 38% of inbound tickets without a human, with a customer satisfaction score of 4.2 out of 5, against 4.3 for human-handled tickets. Handle time fell by half on automated tickets. The main risk is billing disputes, which the model handled poorly; we propose excluding that category in the first phase.
