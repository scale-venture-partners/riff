# Review: the Harbor Field Backpack

After three months of daily commuting, the Harbor Field is the first bag I have not wanted to replace. The laptop sleeve is genuinely protective and the straps stay comfortable on a full load.

It is not perfect. The water bottle pocket is too shallow, and at this price I expected a luggage pass-through. But for the daily train-and-walk commute, it does the job better than bags costing twice as much.
